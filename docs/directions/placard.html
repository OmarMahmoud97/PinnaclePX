Any business's one-page site, set as a well-made printed bill: a banner headline, a heavy rule struck once in the spot colour where there is something to do, margin notes that answer the reader's next question, and one ask pressed into the paper with its undertaking beside it.

Template. Multi-industry; t10-placard if the set grows to ten.

For. The business's next customer, usually on a phone from search, a map listing or a friend's link, who wants to know what the business does, whether it is for them and how to start.

Primary ask. One enquiry, sent from the reply slip at the foot of the page. The label is the business's own first step, and a risk-reversal line sits in the slot with it. Example: Book a sweep, with "A fixed price agreed before we start".

Example. Kestrel Sweeps, a fictional chimney sweep and stove fitter in the Calder Valley.

Proof 5 of 8

Buttons, links and the phone menu

Which thing do I press? Only the ask is filled, and only the ask sits in a slot. It never nudges: hover sinks it 1 px and press 2 px, and the wall's shadow on its top edge deepens. Everything else is a rule or an underline in ink. Every target is at least 44 px; the focus ring is on-surface, 17.39:1.

Proof 6 of 8

The reply slip, in every state

What happens when I press send? Boxed fields, surface on the slip's surface-muted with a 1 px on-surface-muted edge (6.43:1 at worst), labels above, required fields marked in words. Errors are in ink: a 2 px edge, a rule and a sentence, never a new colour. On a visitor's page the send ends in the decision-5 preview note; nothing is sent.

Proof 7 of 8

The pressing: one light, once, across the slot

Is the light worth its bytes? The slot reads without it: the right-hand stage is what phones, reduced motion and every visitor before intent get. From 48rem, after the first pointer move, scroll or key, one light crosses the paper grain and comes to rest above the ask, and the slot's walls answer it.

Waiting for intent and for the stage to be in view.

  1. First paint. The static slot, in CSS at every pixel ratio. The canvas starts as the flat sheet and the walls at 0%, so arming never flashes.
  2. On intent, from 48rem. A pointer move, scroll or key while the stage is in view. The light enters from the upper left.
  3. 0.2 to 1.1 s. It rakes across the paper grain, capped at border, and the slot's walls turn as it passes: the right lip darkens, the left wall lifts.
  4. 1.4 s, at rest. Above and left of the ask; the walls settle deeper than the CSS. Hover, focus or press relights them in 140 ms. Nothing loops.
