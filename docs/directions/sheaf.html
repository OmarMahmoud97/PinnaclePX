      Type

      Familjen Grotesk 700 for display, the wordmark and every figure; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400 and 600 for reading. Both SIL Open Font License 1.1. Every step is clamp() with a rem term; the live size at this width is shown beside each.

      Palette

      Buttons and links

      One label, one filled key. Hover, focus-visible and press all step the fill to brand-deepest, so touch gets every state; press scales to 0.98. The ring is 2 px on-surface at a 3 px offset. A transparent 2 px border keeps the key's edge in forced colours. Every target is at least 44 × 44.

      Questions

      Why is it free?

      The bank pays interest on every pot. You get 3.85% and we keep 0.40%. There is no monthly fee and no fee to take money out.

      Is my money safe if Stonechat closes?

      Your pot is an account in your name at partner bank. If we closed, the money stays there and comes back to you.

      Can I take money out today?

      Yes, any day, with no fee.

      Does it file my tax return?

      No. Stonechat puts the money aside; the return is still yours to file.

      Every ask scrolls to #start with a scroll margin, so the card's top and its action are in view. A reader who scrolls meets the questions first, with the dock's ask pointing down to the card.
