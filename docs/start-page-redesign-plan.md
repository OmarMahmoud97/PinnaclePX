## 1. Brief

The owner, 24 September 2026, verbatim: "now with the same great redesign standards lets redesign and rebuild the questionaire page to match our new amazing site and branding and colour scheme. also ensure it looks great in mobile aswell as look at the home page in mobile as there are a few bits that need optimising for example all of the websites I have built under eachother make it so that the user has to scroll for a really long time to get to the next section. in the hero the sizing of the input field and button needs readjusting and fixing as it doesnt look good. the menu in tablet and movile could do with a nice openeing and closing animation so it looks more premium. the section where the user scrolls through to see the steps of the website build and the mobile screen changes as the user scrolls doesnt make sense on mobile because the user cant see the screen as it scrolls so think of a better way to implement this. and finally as the user scrolls between the top sections in the page the navigation bar animation looks non premium as the show me my three designs button doesnt apear and disapear smoothly if the user scrolls a little too fast".

"The same great redesign standards" are the home page redesign of 23 September 2026, recorded in ADR 0034 and its plan: the hero's ink continued below the hero as a dark stretch (`--ink-foot` `#020a12`), a pooled curve, a wash (`--surface-wash` `#e2eef7`, deep `#c6dcee`), a white run and a dark footer sheet; no hairlines below the hero (separation is a ground, a card, air or a shape); Mona Sans with Instrument Serif italic on exactly three phrases; cards on `--shadow-card` with no border; the colour grammar in `app/globals.css` (blue is the product, glows are light, everything else greyscale); motion under the executor contract with the caps in `CONFIG.motion.caps`; reduced motion keeps finished states and moves colour or opacity only; WCAG 2.2 AA tested by axe; the header a floating glass island once scrolled, with an ink dot under the current section and a full dark sheet on phones.

Part 1 rebuilds `/start`. Part 2 is the phone pass on `/`: the hero's field and button, Work's six stacked tiles, the menu's open and close, the walkthrough's phone, and the header's ask between the top sections. Section 20 is the build order: twelve packages with disjoint files.

The thesis, in five sentences. The visitor arrives from a page whose argument is "see it before you hire", and on `/start` the sketch becomes the page's event: it sits on the hero's own ink, with a cyan pool of light behind the frames, and where the page stacks the pooled curve hangs from it over the light side. On a phone the sketch is a whole backlit phone on the ink beside the brief as a list, as the six sites sit in Work, and the ask rides at the foot of the screen so the next step is never a scroll away. At question five the pool behind the frames takes the visitor's colour as they hover or tap, directly above "Show me my three designs". Once sent, the lights go down: the whole page turns to the ink, the finished sketch glows in their colour, and the call is the one filled button, so the page ends the way the home page ends.
