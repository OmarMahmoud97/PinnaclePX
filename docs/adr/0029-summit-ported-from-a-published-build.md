# Summit, ported from a published build: PrebuiltUI's MediCare as t07

- Status: accepted
- Date: 16 September 2026
- Builds on: ADR 0008 (the template contract), ADR 0023 (the three ports and their rules), ADR 0027 (Ember) and ADR 0028 (Harbor), the two earlier ports from published builds, `docs/template-porting-guide.md`

## Context

The owner asked for PrebuiltUI's hospital template, MediCare, to become `t07`, "not missing any details or animations or anything". As with Restro and Forged, the repository folder (prebuiltui/prebuiltui, `templates/hospital-website-template`, MIT, copyright 2025 PrebuiltUI) has only ever held a README, and the template's published build at medicare.prebuiltui.com is the source: a server-rendered Next.js site whose HTML carries every element and class, whose stylesheet carries its theme, its three fonts and its own rules, and whose two script chunks carry the bar and the footer (one), and all nine blocks of the page as client components with their motion configuration and their data (the other).

The port proceeds under the owner's confirmation of 15 September 2026 that PrebuiltUI's templates are cleared for use in a product that is sold, given for Restro and taken to cover this template from the same publisher and repository.

## Decision

1. **The build is the source, read whole.** The HTML, the stylesheet (Tailwind 4; Geist on the page, Geist Mono loaded and unused, and Urbanist given to every `h1` by a rule of the site's own after the utilities, with no class on any heading to show it; its `orange-500` overridden to `#fe6e00` and its `green-600` to `#00a544`; a `body` colour of `#171717`; a rule sizing the date field's calendar mark to 14px and dimming it) and the two script chunks (the bar's scroll listener at ten pixels and its sheet by state; Lenis at 1.2s with anchors offset by 120px; every block's markup, data and framer-motion props: three springs and one tween, listed under 5) were saved, pretty-printed and read in full. Nothing was taken from a screenshot or from memory. The server payload carries no props here, since every block is a client component; the props were read from the chunk.
2. **Every block is ported, class for class, with the tree the source has.** Eleven blocks in the source's order: the bar and its sheet, the hero, Why choose us, the deck of services, How it works, the facilities, the FAQ, the articles, the appointment form, the closing band and the footer. The source is Tailwind 4, so every utility is kept verbatim (`max-w-360`, `w-144.75`, `h-94.75`, `aspect-370/250`, `text-sm/5.5`, `leading-6.5`, `ring-6`, `bg-black/4`); only its container padding is written out as a recipe, and the two classes on its form fields that Tailwind 4 does not know (`placeholder-text-xs`, `placeholder-zinc-400`), which generated nothing in its own stylesheet, are left off. The deck's cards keep the source's `sticky` and its inline offsets (96px, then 40px more per card), and the template root clips nothing so the cards can stick to the viewport.
3. **The token map for a fixed light palette with many greys.** White is `surface`; slate-50 (the cards, the bands, the hover washes) `surface-muted`; purple-100, the tint of every other service card, `accent`; slate-200 `border` and zinc-200 the same black at 11 percent (within a few units of each other on white); slate-100 (a card under the pointer, the footer's rule, the watermark's outline) the border at half strength; the black buttons `brand-deeper`, their zinc-800 hover `brand-deepest`, and white on them `on-brand`; zinc-600, the body grey, `on-surface-muted`; zinc-800, 700, 500, 400 and 300 the same black at 85, 75, 55, 37 and 17 percent; the orange stars `glow` and the green dot before a reading time `glow-secondary`, both decoration; the black wash under a facility's caption `scrim` and the white on it `on-scrim`. The example's set is the source's values by hand; `?scheme=dark` derives a set from the purple the source tints with.
4. **Three pieces are optional, not absent.** The hero's rating line and its patients, the articles and the form's list of people to see need facts the brief does not hold, so they are `| null`, never written by the copy stage, and filled on the example from the source's own copy. The form's list of departments is the page's own services on a visitor's page (the source's three departments on the example), and the fourth field takes a typed name when there is no list. Thirteen image slots: the hero's photograph, Why choose us's, the closing band's, the six services' and the four facilities'; the three article pictures are example-only.
5. **Motion plays as the source's did, and the page is complete without it.** The source's wrappers used three springs and one tween: stiffness 320, damping 70 and mass 1 for most parts (over critical damping; run to rest as the library runs it, 1.18s for the longest travel of 150px); 240, 70 and 1 for the big headings, the hero's row of portraits, the Why grid and the closing band's parts (1.48s); 300, 30 and 1 for the article cards (under critical damping, 0.36s, overshooting by a fraction); and a half-second tween from two fifths opacity on the hero and the closing band. Each spring is the spring itself, sampled into a `linear()` easing (within a hundredth of the true curve), with the closest cubic bezier as the fallback. Every part plays once, the first time any of it comes into view, with no margin, after its own wait: the hero's parts on a timer, since they are on screen at load; everything below marked shown by a small observer (`sections/reveal.tsx`) and played by the stylesheet with `animation-fill-mode: backwards`, then left alone. Blocks are hidden only under `@media (scripting: enabled)` and with motion allowed. The bar turns to glass ten pixels down by the source's own listener and is clear while the sheet is open; the sheet is the source's own row of links, widening from nothing to the screen over 300ms below md, and is inert only while closed and narrow (a `matchMedia` listener), so the same links stay in the tab order from md; Escape closes it. The FAQ is the source's state accordion, kept as a small client component (`sections/faq.tsx`) because its rows open one at a time and close with the same half-second transition they open with, which native disclosure rows cannot; each button carries `aria-expanded` and each closed panel is inert. A facility's caption slides up from below its photograph over 300ms as the photograph swells over 700ms, under the pointer from md and always up below it; an article's picture swells over 500ms; every arrow nudges right; a portrait lifts a pixel. The source's Lenis is not ported (templates cannot import it, and the site's own instance glides on previews); its 120px anchor offset is `scroll-mt-30` on every anchored block.

## Consequences

- Seven templates are ready; the selector chooses three of seven per submission. The registry test's "template without a contract" case moved from `t07-summit` to `t08-vector`.
- Verified on 16 September 2026 against the published build: every one of the eleven blocks matches the source's rendered height to the pixel at 1535px and at 390px, except Why choose us, whose heading is one line shorter on the example because "Kestrel" is a shorter name than "MediCare" (with the source's own words put into the port's heading it measures exactly the source's, 120px and 180px, at both widths); the screenshot pairs match (the source's pairs show elements mid-entrance, since its motion library ignores reduced motion). With motion allowed: the hero brightens and its parts rise on load (the headline over 1.48s); a block below the fold is hidden until it comes into view, then plays once after its wait (the deck from 100px, a question row from 150px, an article card over 0.36s, the band from two fifths), and all 47 blocks below the hero are marked after a scroll through; the bar is clear at the top and glass twenty pixels down; the deck's first three cards stick at 96, 136 and 176 pixels; a facility's caption is 379px below its cell and clear until the pointer, then at its foot and full as the photograph scales to 1.05; the FAQ opens a row to 160px over 0.5s, closes it when another opens, and closes the open one when pressed again; on a phone the sheet is nothing wide and inert when closed, the screen wide and live when open with the bar clear behind it, and Escape closes it, and the captions are always up; under reduced motion, and with JavaScript off, every block is visible at once and nothing animates; at 1920 and 390 in both schemes there is no horizontal overflow and no console error.
- axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) reports one rule, colour contrast, on the source's own zinc-400: the two lists' and the date field's text and the footer's small print and its four small links, at 2.58:1 on white (eight nodes; 3.14:1 on the derived dark set). Kept as the source has them, since the owner asks for the source's look; raising the alpha is the owner's call and joins the other inherited findings in `docs/claims-register.md`.
- Deviations from the source, all deliberate: the section headings are `h2` and the service card titles `h3` (the source has nine `h1`s); the FAQ and the articles carry ids (`faq`, `articles`) so links and the comparison can name them; the logo links to the top of the page, not to prebuiltui.com; the form's labels carry `uppercase` (the source typed its own in capitals), are tied to their fields, and the fields are named so a mail message carries them; the form posts to the owner's email as a mail message when it is known and otherwise leads to the closing band (the source's posted nowhere); the form's button hovers to `brand-deepest` like every other button (the source's hovered to zinc-900, fifteen units darker than the others' zinc-800); a facility's caption link leads to the form (the source's led nowhere), and its text turns to the scrim's colour under the pointer rather than zinc-800, so it stays dark on the white button on a dark set; the "Our Services" column in the footer is links to the services (the source's were plain paragraphs with a hover colour); the FAQ's icon disc is a `span`, not a `div` inside a `span`; the "Designed by PrebuiltUI" line is not ported and the legal line is "© year, legal name. All rights reserved." with the photographers' credit after it; the page's default text is `on-surface` rather than the source's `#171717`, which no element on the page shows since every text carries its own grey.
- `docs/claims-register.md` row "A person designs every layout" still needs the owner's decision: six of the seven ready layouts were designed by other people and adapted here.
- The porting guide gains: read the stylesheet past the utilities for element rules and confirm every heading's rendered face; keep a state accordion when its rows must close with the transition they open with; keep a sticky deck's offsets and clip nothing above it; make a shared menu row inert only while closed and narrow.
