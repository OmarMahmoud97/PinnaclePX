# Three templates ported from open-source layouts: Monolith, Meridian and Atlas

- Status: accepted
- Date: 5 September 2026
- Builds on: ADR 0008 (the template contract, Aurora)

## Context

Aurora was the only ready template, and the page promises three designs. The owner found three free landing-page repositories and asked that they become `t02` to `t04`, keeping each layout exactly as it is and making every word, picture and colour on the page come from the visitor's form. All three are MIT licensed, so they may be used in a product that is sold, provided the copyright notices are kept.

| Template       | Source                                                                                | Author              | Stack in the source           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| `t02-monolith` | [leoMirandaa/shadcn-landing-page](https://github.com/leoMirandaa/shadcn-landing-page) | Leopoldo Miranda    | Vite, React, shadcn, Tailwind |
| `t03-meridian` | [nobruf/shadcn-landing-page](https://github.com/nobruf/shadcn-landing-page)           | Bruno Felipy        | Next.js, shadcn, Tailwind     |
| `t04-atlas`    | [RSurya99/nefa](https://github.com/RSurya99/nefa)                                     | Rafli Surya Pratama | Nuxt 2, Vue, Tailwind         |

The sources cannot be used as they stand: their copy is hard-coded, their colours are hex values and Tailwind palette classes, their type is a font each, and two of them ship Radix, a carousel, a marquee, a form library and a theme toggle. The template rules (ADR 0008 and the lint boundaries) forbid all of that. A first port on 5 September 2026 rebuilt the layouts loosely and left out every section that needed a fact the brief does not hold; the owner rejected it, since the pages had to be indistinguishable from the sources. This is the second port, done class for class against the sources running locally.

## Decision

1. **Every source section is ported, class for class.** Each section is a React server component that carries the source's Tailwind classes translated to the tokens (`bg-background` to `bg-surface`, `bg-card` to `bg-accent`, `bg-muted` and `bg-secondary` to `bg-surface-muted`, `text-primary` to `text-brand-deeper`, and so on), its shadcn primitives (card, badge, button, input, separator) written out in `styles.ts`, its icons (lucide by name, the sources' own illustration and brand-mark files, Material Design Icons by path) and its own stylesheet rules in a scoped file. The two shadcn sources were compared with the originals run from their repositories at a 1535px viewport, section by section, until every section's rendered height matched to the pixel; Nefa likewise, run under Nuxt 2.
2. **Sections that need facts the brief does not hold are optional, not absent.** Testimonials, team members, prices, a newsletter, market tables, exchange rows and partner logos are `| null` in the content object. The copy stage never writes them and `assemble` sets them null, so a visitor's page is complete without them and claims nothing; the example content fills them from the sources' own placeholder copy and pictures, so the layout can be reviewed whole against its source. Where the source's shape could carry real content it does: the sponsors' rows carry labels for what the business covers, Monolith's hero cards carry the brand's statement and profile, Atlas's card under the hero carries three columns of words when there are no tables, its boxed row carries the owner's statement when there is no exchange, and Meridian's contact rows carry the steps.
3. **Forms send to the owner.** `TemplateAssets` gains `email`, the lead's address, which the concept page reads with the submission. Meridian's contact form and the newsletter forms post to it as a mail message, which is what the source's contact form did; without an address a form leads to the page's ask.
4. **The example pages carry the sources' own themes.** Each example page writes a token set by hand from the source's theme (the shadcn `.dark` and `:root` blocks converted to hex; Nefa's fixed colours) and sets the source's font (the system stack, Inter, Poppins), so the example renders in the source's colours and type. The pipeline still derives a brand's tokens; a template never knows its colours. The two glow tokens carry the sources' accent gradients (Monolith's pink and cyan headline words, Meridian's magenta, Nefa's cyan and first blue), so a brand's set recolours those too.
5. **Tailwind 4 differences are written out, never left to override order.** Tailwind 4 orders conflicting utilities by name, not by position in a class string, and its `space-y` puts its margin on the opposite child from Tailwind 3. Every variant the sources reach by overriding (a badge at text-sm, a title at text-lg, a coloured description, a round icon button, a header without padding) is a named recipe, and the sources' `space-*` is written as `[&>*+*]:mt-*`. Two of the sources' class-merger effects are reproduced on purpose: `leading-none` is dropped when a card title is resized, and their `text-md` typo drops `text-sm`, so those descriptions render at the base size as they do in the sources.
6. **Motion is CSS and the page is complete without it.** Each template's one client component is its phone menu (Atlas's whole header, which the source toggled by state), plus Meridian's carousel buttons. Monolith's sliding glow, its scroll-revealed back-to-top button, Meridian's marquee and hover menu, and Nefa's directional entrances (its scroll-animation library's fades and flips with their delays) are keyframes in the scoped stylesheets, off under reduced motion. Accordions are native disclosure elements with the sources' timing.
7. **Attribution.** `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` carries the three MIT notices, which the licence requires wherever substantial portions are used, and names the sources of the example pictures.

## Consequences

- Four templates are ready, one more than the page's promise of three, so `LAUNCH_GATE` passes and the selector chooses three of four per submission.
- Verified on 5 September 2026 against the sources running locally: every section of every template matches its original's rendered size at 1535px, and the visuals were compared pair by pair. At 1920 and 390 px there is no horizontal overflow and no console error. axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) is clean on Atlas; on Monolith it reports the source's own FAQ link, which is told apart by colour alone, and on Meridian the source's own orange-on-white buttons, which are the example theme's colours and not a derived set's (the tokens stage brings every declared pair to AA). Both are kept as the sources have them.
- Deviations from the sources, all deliberate: the sources' theme toggles and repository links are not rendered (a business page has neither); Meridian's Discord mark is a speech-bubbles mark; the example portraits from pravatar.cc and Unsplash stand in where a source loaded a person's GitHub avatar; Atlas's ornaments are drawn in the brand hues rather than the source's fixed blue and purple.
- `docs/claims-register.md` row "A person designs every layout" still needs the owner's decision: three of the four ready layouts were designed by other people and adapted here.
- The registry test's "template without a contract" case moved from `t02-monolith` to `t05-ember`.
- Six more placeholders remain (`t05` to `t10`). The owner has said more repositories will follow; each takes the same shape, the same sieve for invented facts, and the same section-by-section comparison against the source running locally.
